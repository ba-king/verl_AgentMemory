When preserving a relative time expression from the current dialogue, keep its stated wording and also store data.reference_date from current_session.date in the same record. This date anchors when the expression was said; it is not a due date or a completion date. Do not invent a precise deadline from an ambiguous expression. When retaining an older relative-time fact, retain its original reference date instead of re-anchoring it to the current session. If the relevant public reference date is unavailable, leave it unknown rather than inventing one.
